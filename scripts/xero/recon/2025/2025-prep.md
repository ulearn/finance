### Pre-Flight Checklist

Before starting each month's reconciliation:

1. **Check for Outstanding Sales Invoices** (Groups)
   - Review unpaid invoices in Xero
   - Note invoice numbers, amounts, and payment terms (10% / 45% / 45%)
   - Watch for matching payments in bank feed

2. **Review This Month's Section Below**
   - Outstanding invoices to watch for
   - Known unusual transactions
   - Special instructions

3. **Verify Bank Rules**
   - Ensure Xero bank rules are still active
   - Priority 1: Use bank rules if they apply
